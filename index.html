Sound Guide

Deep drone
Pit / Airstream temperature. Pitch rises as the pit gets hotter.
Soft mid tone
Rib probe 1. Tracks that probe’s temperature.
Second mid tone
Rib probe 2. Creates gentle beating when it differs from probe 1.
Higher quieter tone
Rib probe 3. Adds density and moves with the third rack.
Noise / texture
Drive (fan) output. Grows louder and brighter when the fan runs harder.
The texture is designed to stay low and continuous. Once you learn it, you can hear the state of the cook without looking at the numbers.
