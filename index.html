Sound Guide

Deep drone
Air / pit temperature. Continuous. Rising or falling produces a gentle bend (notice).
Bird calls
Only live meat probes sing. Each has a distinct voice. Calls every 1–2 minutes. Higher temp → higher pitch. Ending rises if that probe is climbing, falls if dropping.
Noise texture
Drive / fan output. Grows with fan speed.
Total Heat
Running integral of temperature × time (°F·minutes) for each meat probe.
Falling below 180 °F
The falling notice on the air drone becomes stronger and more frequent.
Birds stay silent until a probe reports a live reading after you press Start. Unplugged probes do not sing.
